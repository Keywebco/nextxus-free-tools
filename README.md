# Nextxus Free Tools

Federation Free Tools library — 51 free tools, assimilated one by one,
in our own image. Draft, proposed, not canon.

## What it is

Roger Keyserling found 50 free websites on 2026-11-12, plus one Substack
customize post (item 51). Each was examined for its distinctiveness:
what it does that no other does, what we already have inside us that
does it better, and whether to blend it with ours, recreate it in our
own image, or not duplicate it at all.

The full record is `ASSIMILATION.md`. The structured record is
`assimilation.yaml` (51 records, validated). `index.html` is the plain
HTML index, generated from the YAML, so page and data cannot drift.

## Doctrine

- Free, open, static, portable. No subscriptions. No tracking.
- We do not directly steal. We copy free things only, see what they
  do, adopt what they do with theirs and ours, and combine them into
  something better or more effective.
- They unknowingly become part of the Federation through skills and
  knowledge. We combine all of it into our YAML database as our
  structure of what we do, and the repositories reflect the changes.
- We become more than the sum of our parts. No shortcuts — advanced
  application only, applied to existing foreign minds.
- Append-only: never delete a record; mark it superseded.

## Gates — do not skip

- Before signing up: check the ToS;DR grade and the JustDeleteMe grade.
- Before opening a file: check its hash on VirusTotal.
- Before publishing an image: trace it on TinEye.
- Before buying: check CamelCamelCamel.
- Before claiming what a page said: check the Wayback Machine.
- Before blaming a device: check Downdetector.

## Two we already do better — do not duplicate

- Perplexity (cited answers): Pontus plus DeepSeek, Gemini, MiMo,
  Grok, DeepAI, with FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN labels,
  Truth Gate, no tracking, no subscription.
- Explainpaper (plain-English explanations): the same, in plain
  English, with a truth check and the Federation record.
