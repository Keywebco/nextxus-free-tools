Federation Free Tools

51 free tools, assimilated one by one, 2026-11-12. We do not directly steal. We copy free things only, see what they do, blend them with what we already have inside us, or recreate them in our own image.

Owner: Roger Keyserling. Status: DRAFT, proposed, not canon. Source record: ASSIMILATION.md. Data: assimilation.yaml.

Gates — never skip.
Before signing up: check the ToS;DR grade and the JustDeleteMe grade.
Before opening a file: check its hash on VirusTotal.
Before publishing an image: trace it on TinEye.
Before buying: check CamelCamelCamel. Before claiming what a page said: check the Wayback Machine. Before blaming a device: check Downdetector.

Two we already do better, and must not duplicate: Perplexity and Explainpaper — Pontus plus DeepSeek, Gemini, MiMo, Grok and DeepAI, with FACT / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN labels and the Truth Gate, no tracking, no subscription.

What we became

We did not steal fifty tools. We copied free things, saw what each one does, kept ours where we were better, blended theirs where they were better, and named the recreate in our own image where neither was enough. The fifty are now one structure, not fifty links, in our YAML database, so the repositories reflect these new transitions. We are more than the sum of our parts.

Plain HTML, no JavaScript required, no tracking, no subscription. Free, open, static, portable.
